House Equity

Compare ways to access home equity earlier — calibrated HECM (published PLF tables), private RM-style, HELOC, cash-out refinance & invest, or sell and rent. All strategies share a mortgage runway until the access year. Loan math is nominal; charts show today’s dollars. Money inputs use $000s like the rest of the app; rates use percent. HECM proceeds use HUD PLF / MCA / 2% initial MIP for sizing; draws stay educational — not a lender quote or compliance calculator. Sale costs include commission, other closing costs, and a simple capital-gains tax (not a tax-prep tool). Home appreciation uses your expected real rate plus Monte Carlo noise (~2% σ).
